## H3 — τ = 1: two-sided bound and the √T law (2026-11-11)

**Hypothesis.** For independent states and γ ≤ γ_ε, two-point methods of radius γ have threshold Θ(εγ√T/(dR)); the relevant parameter is the oscillation of δ on scale γ.
**Verdict: confirmed in a refined form.** Proven with explicit constants for the class A_γ^2pt (difference feedback at radius ≤ γ, direction laws fixed in advance; contains ZO-SGD), convex M-Lipschitz f, i.i.d. states with arbitrary initial law, γ ≤ ε/(4M), ε < MR/8, T ≥ 1 + 16d²M²R²/((d−1)ε²), expectation variant: εγ√(T−1)/(4dR) ≤ Δ_max^{E,2pt,γ} ≤ 32π·εγ√T/(dR) (ZO-SGD idles in round 1; √T with a stationary start). Lower side: Δ_cp^E(24) ≥ 1.034·Mγ/√d (probability variant at C = 24/β²). Upper side, separately: Δ_cp^{E,2pt,γ}(C) ≤ 32π√C·Mγ/√d, which closes H1.5d for τ = 1 and A_γ^2pt. Not covered: smooth f with ε/(4M) < γ ≤ √(ε/L) (lower side).
**Refinements.** (i) The ±1 cell noise of H3.2 is decodable for Δ > 2Mγ (any f) and at every Δ on the linear hypercube; our technique uses a law with finite Fisher information (Hellinger replaces KL); necessity not shown. (ii) Any algorithm: Δ_max^E ≤ 71·ε√T/√d. Cited bandit rates (losses in [0,1], not re-checked), rescaled to the range of f, help only at budgets T ≳ C_b d^{k−1}T0 (then for MR/ε ≳ d^{k/2−1}); at complexity-preserving budgets they give nothing. "Bessel part blocks bandit methods in the fixed-γ class" is refuted by ball averaging. (iii) Δ → ω_δ(2γ)/2 on both sides; Λ-Lipschitz noise costs M → M+Λ; smooth noise costs ∝ 1−Φ_d(2γ/ℓ) (knee at γ/√d); rounding with fresh offset has ω̄ ≤ u/2.
**Numerics (5 runs).** E3.1: collapse in Δ/√T confirmed on the progress subsets of the 4 main problems (a = 0.49–0.52, CIs contain ½); all-points fits a = 0.50–0.56, d = 80 CIs exclude ½ by ≤ 0.03; ±1 variant meets the refutation criterion (a = 0.67), equal-ω̄ rerun a = 0.52 (all) but 0.63 [0.52, 0.77] on its progress subset (unresolved). E3.2: estimator law exact to 0.6%; optimisation cost at ℓ = γ 0.18/0.07, not 1; factor-2 criterion fails for d = 80. E3.4: sanity check only. E3.5: decoded ±1 = noiseless to 1e-16. E3.3 not run.
**Implications.** The threshold depends on the algorithm class Δ_max refers to (for arbitrary algorithms it is only bracketed); H2's first branch at τ = 1 holds from both sides for A_γ^2pt; nothing is concluded about H2's second branch; the Hellinger/coupling machinery is expected to apply to H5 (not checked).
